sort1 uses: Bubble Sort

How do you know?:
Sort 1 is a bubble sort, since sorted list of numbers had a progression of Omega(n),
whereas other lists has taken O(n^2) progression

sort2 uses: Merge Sort

How do you know?:
Sort 2 was the fastest which is O(nlog n) progression, means it is divide and conquer algorithm (Merge Sort)

sort3 uses: Selection Sort

How do you know?:
Sort 3 has taken "the average time" to sort all the lists with milliseconds in difference between each other,
given it O(n^2) time complexity across all cases
